# KharchaTrack Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 11 November 2026

KharchaTrack is an offline-first personal income and expense tracker.

## Information stored on your device

- Transaction titles, categories, amounts and dates are stored locally on your device.
- Monthly budget, selected currency, theme, reminder preferences and app settings are stored locally.
- PIN protection is stored using Android secure storage. If you enable biometric unlock, authentication is handled by your device's operating system; KharchaTrack does not receive your fingerprint or face data.
- Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

KharchaTrack does not currently provide user accounts, cloud sync or a developer-operated transaction database.

## Backups and exports

If you choose Export CSV, Create Backup or Restore Backup, the app creates or reads a file at your direction. You decide where exported files are saved or shared. Backup files can contain sensitive financial information, so keep them private and secure.

## Advertising and data sharing

KharchaTrack does not display advertisements and does not include an advertising SDK. The app does not send transaction records to a KharchaTrack server.

## Your choices

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Exported backup and CSV files must be deleted separately from the locations where you saved or shared them.

## Children's privacy

KharchaTrack is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The app owner must configure any advertising and audience settings in accordance with applicable laws and platform policies before public distribution.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The current version will be published in this repository.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method provided on the KharchaTrack repository page.
